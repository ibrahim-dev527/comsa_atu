Our Story

About COMSA

Discover the mission, values, and legacy that drive the Communications Students' Association at Accra Technical University.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯
Mission

Our Mission

To foster academic excellence, professional development, and personal growth among Communication Studies students at ATU. We create a supportive, inclusive environment where every student's voice matters — providing platforms for skill development, networking, and leadership that prepare our members for successful careers in communications and beyond.

🌟
Vision

Our Vision

To be the most vibrant, impactful, and well-organised students' association in Ghana — one that sets the standard for student governance, academic engagement, and community service. We envision a COMSA synonymous with excellence, integrity, and innovation, producing communicators who are the heartbeat of Ghana's professional landscape.

Our Journey

The History of COMSA

From humble beginnings to a thriving association — every chapter has shaped who we are today.

2004

Foundation

COMSA was formally established by pioneering students of the Communications Department at ATU, laying the groundwork for a community dedicated to academic and professional excellence.

2008

First Association Week

COMSA hosted its inaugural Association Week, bringing together students, faculty, and industry professionals for a week of celebration, learning, and networking.

2013

Expanded Leadership Structure

The association expanded its executive framework to include portfolios for welfare, women's representation (WOCOM), and part-time student representation, ensuring comprehensive coverage for all members.

2019

Community Outreach Programme

COMSA launched its first major community outreach initiative, extending beyond campus to bring communication skills workshops to secondary schools in Greater Accra.

2025

"The All Inclusive Administration"

Under President Abdul Jalil Sani, COMSA enters a new era committed to representation, transparency, and excellence for every member of the COMSA family.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These principles guide our decisions, shape our culture, and define our legacy.

🤝

Inclusivity

Every student belongs. We create an environment where every voice — regardless of background, year, or programme — is valued, heard, and represented.

🏆

Excellence

We set high standards and pursue them relentlessly — in academics, events, leadership, and every aspect of association life.

💎

Integrity

Transparency, honesty, and accountability underpin everything we do. We lead by example and hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards.

💡

Innovation

We embrace creative thinking and fresh approaches to problem-solving. COMSA is a space where new ideas are welcomed, tested, and celebrated.

🌱

Growth

We invest in the development of every member — providing opportunities to learn, lead, and grow personally and professionally throughout their time at ATU.

🔗

Unity

Together, we are stronger. COMSA thrives on the collective energy and dedication of its members — one community, one purpose, one voice.

Department

Department of Communication Studies

The Department of Communication Studies at Accra Technical University is one of Ghana's leading programmes in media, journalism, and strategic communication. COMSA serves as the student voice and social engine of this department.

Leadership Philosophy

Servant Leadership at Its Finest

COMSA's leadership philosophy is rooted in servant leadership — the belief that the greatest leaders are those who put the needs of their members first. Our executives are not above the membership; they are the membership, elevated to serve.

"Leadership is not about titles. It's about impact, influence, and inspiring others to be the best versions of themselves."

— COMSA Leadership Motto
