About COMSA

Our Story & Mission

Who We Are

Building Exceptional Communicators

COMSA is the official students' association for the Department of Liberal Studies and Communication Technology at Accra Technical University. We empower students through leadership, networking, skills development, and vibrant academic and social engagement.

🎯

To promote academic excellence, foster leadership, and create a unified platform for all communication students at ATU to thrive and grow.

Our History

From PRESA to COMSA

For many years, the association was known as the Public Relations Students' Association (PRESA), reflecting the department's composition at the time.

However, under the Sani–Oti-led Administration, a bold and forward-thinking decision was made to rename the association the Communication Students' Association (COMSA). This change recognized the department's evolving structure, which now included Multimedia students and was preparing to welcome Graphic Communication students.

The rebranding was driven by a vision of inclusivity — creating an association where every communication student feels represented, valued, and a true part of the community. Rather than identifying with a single discipline, COMSA was introduced as an umbrella identity that embraces all programmes within the department.

With this historic transition, the association was officially rebranded, marking the beginning of a more united, inclusive, and progressive era for the department.

As part of the association's rebranding, two historic identity symbols were introduced — the first-ever official COMSA Lapel Pin and the official COMSA Association Cloth. These additions were designed to strengthen the association's identity, foster unity, and give members a greater sense of belonging and pride.
