The Department

Department of Liberal Studies & Communication Technology

The Department of Liberal Studies and Communication Technology at Accra Technical University is one of Ghana's leading centres for communication education. The department emphasises competency-based learning and practice-oriented approaches, in line with ATU's mission to produce work-ready graduates capable of offering technical and creative solutions in a dynamic business environment.

Uniquely positioned at the intersection of communication, technology, and consumer psychology, the department integrates emerging trends such as drone communication, digital analytics, and multimedia branding. Students engage in hands-on learning through a media lab and an industry-standard multimedia studio that bridges theory with real-world application.

About This Programme

The MTech in Communication and Brand Management is an innovative, industry-aligned graduate programme designed to equip students with advanced knowledge and practical skills in strategic communication, branding, and digital media. The programme integrates emerging trends such as drone communication, digital analytics, and multimedia branding. Students engage in hands-on learning through a media lab and industry-standard multimedia studio, bridging theory with real-world application.

Programme Objectives
✅Design and conduct qualitative and quantitative research to generate insights into consumer behaviour and brand performance.
✅Acquire technical expertise to analyse communication data using modern technological and analytical tools.
✅Apply multimedia technology in solving real-world communication and branding challenges.
✅Make informed, strategic decisions in various professional communication settings.

About This Programme

The BTech in Public Relations with Digital Communication enables graduates to explore the developmental role of public relations in society and start their own businesses. This programme is a response to the growing demand in Ghana for professionals trained in communication-related careers. The media and financial industries in Ghana have experienced significant growth, creating an increased need for employees trained in PR and digital communication skills.

Programme Objectives
✅Prepare students for careers requiring professional training in Public Relations.
✅Equip graduates for entry-level or intermediate positions in the PR-related industry.
✅Provide students with skills to diagnose and solve PR problems effectively.
✅Motivate students to explore the developmental roles of digital communication in society.
✅Enable students to develop and manage their own businesses in communications.

About This Programme

The 4-Year BTech in Graphic Communication trains students in the art and science of visual communication. Combining creativity with technical skill, graduates produce compelling visual content across print, digital, and broadcast platforms. The programme covers design principles, typography, branding, and professional digital design tools used across the industry.

Key Learning Areas
🎨Graphic design principles, typography, and layout design for print and digital media.
💻Industry-standard design software including Adobe Creative Suite and other digital tools.
📦Branding, packaging design, and corporate visual identity development.
🌐Web design, UI/UX fundamentals, and digital content production.

About This Programme

The 2-Year Diploma in Public Relations is designed to provide a balance of theory, research, and practice in PR. Students develop an understanding of the role and effect of Public Relations and the allied communication disciplines of Marketing and Advertising within business, social, and political arenas. Critical thinking, creative problem-solving, and decision-justification skills are developed throughout.

On Completion, Students Will Have
✅Developed an understanding of the roles and functions of public relations in various practice settings.
✅Developed critical analysis and decision-making skills for managing public relations situations.
✅Gained practical skills including writing, public speaking, audiovisual development, and broadcast media production.
✅Developed the ability to work independently and in groups to meet specific objectives.
✅Begun to develop a professional identity in the field of public relations.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These principles guide our decisions, shape our culture, and define COMSA's legacy at ATU.

🤝

Inclusivity

Every student belongs. We create an environment where every voice — regardless of background, year, or programme — is valued, heard, and represented.

🏆

Excellence

We set high standards and pursue them relentlessly — in academics, events, leadership, and every aspect of association life. Good enough is never our standard.

💎

Integrity

Transparency, honesty, and accountability underpin everything we do. We lead by example and hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards at all times.

💡

Innovation

We embrace creative thinking and fresh approaches to problem-solving. COMSA is a space where new ideas are welcomed, tested, and celebrated openly.

🌱

Growth

We invest in the development of every member — providing opportunities to learn, lead, and grow personally and professionally throughout their time at ATU.

🔗

Unity

Together, we are stronger. COMSA thrives on the collective energy and dedication of its members — one community, one purpose, one voice.

Leadership Philosophy

Servant Leadership at Its Finest

COMSA's leadership philosophy is rooted in servant leadership — the belief that the greatest leaders put the needs of their members first. Our executives are not above the membership; they are the membership, elevated to serve.

"Leadership is not about titles. It's about impact, influence, and inspiring others to be the best versions of themselves."

— COMSA Leadership Motto
